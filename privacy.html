Privacy Policy

How we handle accounting information accessed by our authorized internal financial application.

Effective date: October 17, 2026  |  Operator: Vines Medical PC

This Privacy Policy describes how Vines Financial Agent (the “Application”), operated by Vines Medical PC (“we,” “our,” or “us”), accesses, processes, stores, and protects information obtained in connection with authorized QuickBooks Online use.

1. Scope and intended users

The Application is an internal financial and accounting support system for authorized business personnel. It is not offered as a public consumer product. This policy covers information handled through its QuickBooks Online connection and related business-financial workflows. It does not replace Intuit's own privacy notices or the company's other applicable privacy policies.

2. Information the Application may access

When a QuickBooks administrator authorizes the connection, the Application may retrieve accounting and financial records within the approved QuickBooks company, including:

  • Chart of accounts, balances, general-ledger entries, financial statements, and transaction details.
  • Invoices, bills, payments, bank-account and credit-card transaction records, and supporting accounting metadata.
  • Vendor, customer, and employee/payroll-related financial fields to the extent present in the authorized QuickBooks records and needed for approved tasks.
  • Company identification and connection metadata, including the QuickBooks company identifier and OAuth credentials required to maintain authorized access.

Access to information depends on the permissions granted through Intuit and the functionality made available by the operator. Although the initial integration is intended for read-only analysis, Intuit's accounting OAuth scope can encompass broader API capabilities; application-level controls govern which operations the agent is permitted to perform.

3. How information is used

We use authorized QuickBooks data to prepare and review financial reports, reconcile and evaluate transactions, identify potential anomalies or missing support, perform bookkeeping research, prepare draft entries and workpapers, and support internal accounting and tax-document organization. We may maintain operational logs and source references needed to investigate errors and validate findings.

AI-assisted analysis is intended to run on infrastructure managed by the operator. We do not intentionally send QuickBooks accounting records to external generative-AI model providers as part of the initial local workflow. Communications with Intuit's services are required to authenticate and retrieve QuickBooks data.

4. Storage and protection

The Application is designed to operate on a locally managed Linux system. Where business documents are used, the operator may access them through a separately secured, read-only Synology NAS financial share. OAuth credentials and token data are stored in access-restricted local configuration or token-storage files; they are not published on this website.

Controls include limited-user access, segregated document sources, and restrictions on unapproved changes to accounting records. No electronic system is completely risk-free, and we do not guarantee absolute security. Security practices may evolve as the Application is developed.

5. Sharing and third parties

Information is exchanged with Intuit as necessary for QuickBooks authorization and API access. Business-financial information may be disclosed to authorized accounting or tax advisers or to service providers where separately approved and lawful. We do not sell QuickBooks information to data brokers or use it for behavioral advertising.

This public legal-notices website is hosted using GitHub Pages. It does not itself request, collect, or store QuickBooks records or OAuth credentials. GitHub may process visitor access information under its own policies; these pages have no intentionally embedded third-party analytics or tracking scripts.

6. Retention and deletion

Accounting records, extracted workpapers, audit logs, and related materials are retained only as needed for legitimate business, accounting, tax, legal, security, and recordkeeping purposes, subject to applicable retention requirements. Disconnecting QuickBooks stops future authorized API retrieval once credentials are revoked or invalidated, but does not automatically delete business records that must be retained for lawful purposes.

When data is no longer required, the operator can remove stored connection credentials and applicable derived records in accordance with retention obligations and approved deletion procedures. Backups may persist for a limited period in accordance with applicable backup and retention practices.

7. Disconnecting QuickBooks and requests

An authorized QuickBooks administrator may revoke the Application's access using the connection-management options offered by Intuit or contact the operator for assistance. Authorized individuals may also ask questions about what data is processed, request correction of inaccurate derived information, or request deletion when appropriate and legally permitted.

8. Sensitive information

The Application is intended for business-financial analysis, not management of medical records or clinical care. Clinical patient information should not be deliberately uploaded or entered into the Application. Financial records may nevertheless contain personal information; such information should be processed only where necessary and subject to applicable organizational and legal safeguards. This policy is not a representation of HIPAA compliance.

9. Changes

We may update this policy as the Application's capabilities, integrations, or applicable requirements change. The effective date above will identify the current version. Material operational changes should be reviewed before the corresponding data processing begins.
